Privacy Policy

Cadence is a personal, self-hosted task and planning app. This instance is run by its owner for their own use. It shows no ads, sells no data and shares nothing with third parties.

What Cadence stores

Everything you enter (tasks, projects, notes, habits) is stored on the server that runs this instance and nowhere else.

Google Calendar and Google Tasks

When you connect a Google account, Cadence asks for access to Google Calendar and Google Tasks to sync them with your Cadence tasks in both directions. It keeps:

  • the OAuth tokens, encrypted, to sync without asking again;
  • copies of your calendar events and Google tasks, and the links between them and your Cadence tasks.

This data is used only to show and sync your own calendar and tasks. It is not used for advertising, not sold and not shared. Cadence's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnecting Google in your profile revokes the token and deletes every Google copy and link kept in Cadence. Your Cadence tasks stay, and so does everything already in your Google account. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions.
